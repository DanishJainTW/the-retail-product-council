What every project inherits

The base holds what is true across retail and no client product. Every project starts from a copy of it.

Thirteen retail areasWhat each area covers, its usual systems, its expert and the cases behind it. 42 global case studiesFilter by region or problem. Every fact shows its source's own words. Failure librarySeven antipatterns, sixteen failure modes and 76 real failures. 135 retail termsSearch by term, abbreviation or area.

Plus fourteen retail expert archetypes, thirty-six reference players and fourteen user persona templates, all on the Technical page.

How the base was built

  • Public sources only. Every fact cites a source in the manifest with its date. Facts older than six months are marked stale.
  • Quoted, then checked. A fact carries the source's own words, and the citation check confirms they appear in the research note.
  • Lessons are inferences. Each case's lesson is tagged inferred, with its reasoning, and comes with a transfer check.
  • Failures are directional. Loot Drop's figures are AI-assisted. The base uses their ranking and mechanisms, never a percentage, in a verdict.
  • No client material. Client sources stay in client projects. Only what is true across retail comes back to the base.

Known gaps

Said plainly, so nobody mistakes a gap for a finding.

  • Competitor evidence packs are not built yet. Reference players point to case studies until /ingest --competitor builds a pack.
  • Retail experts are drafts that no practitioner has checked yet.
  • Catalogue and listing, control tower, pricing and performance marketing have no sourced case study yet.
  • Loot Drop rows 1 to 200 of the retail filter and its Personal Care deep dive were not captured.
  • User personas are templates. Each client project fills them from its own research.
