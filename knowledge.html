The scout runs first, and alone

How the council fetches context before anyone else speaks.

  1. The orchestrator states which facts the decision needs
  2. context-scout runs by itself and returns a Context Pack with a coverage verdict
  3. The orchestrator gates it: sends a thin pack back once, and asks you about any conflict the decision depends on before a panel runs
  4. The orchestrator writes the panel brief from the pack, naming the gaps so no panel assumes them
  5. Panels run in parallel with each other, on that one brief
  6. Citations are checked, then the options and the recommendation are written

A panel briefed before the facts arrive fills the gap with a guess, and a guess with a citation attached is worse than silence.

Context before questions

The council never asks you something it already knows. Before any question, the scout looks up everything your request names: features, personas, teams, systems, partners, regions, terms and figures, in the knowledge base, past sessions and analysis documents. For each intake topic it reports answered, partial or not in context.

What happens to a topic or question, by what the context holds.
Context saysWhat you see
AnsweredNot asked. Shown under "What I already know" with its source, so you can correct it
PartialA question for the missing piece only, quoting what is known
Not in contextA full question with a default

The global and failure lenses

Two habits the retail base adds to every session.

Global lensFor any problem, the council checks the problem index first: who solved or failed at this, in which region, and does it transfer? It names the region. A case is a perspective, never a template. Failure lensThe Premortem builds its story from the failure library: the most likely antipattern, the nearest failure modes and the nearest real case from the same region. Loot Drop figures there are AI-assisted and directional only.
