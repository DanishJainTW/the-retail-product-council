Selling media and buying it

T05 and T13

T05 is the side that sells the media: a retailer or marketplace running an ad business. T13 is the side that buys it: a brand, seller or aggregator spending across marketplaces, Meta and Google, and quick commerce. The same auction looks different from each side, so a retail media question that touches the advertiser should hear both.

Checked in every area

  • Regional difference. The same problem plays out differently by region. Check context/20-curated/case-studies/problem-index.md before answering, and name the region a pattern comes from.
  • Data and AI. Every track uses customer, product or operational data. Agentic commerce is a live theme in vendor messaging (see the Criteo case). Treat vendor claims as claims.
  • Regulation. Consumer protection, data privacy, payments rules and marketplace rules differ by country. Mark the jurisdiction or .
  • Unit economics. Fees, take rates, delivery cost per order and ad revenue as a share of sales decide most retail bets. Use figures only from a source.
