Meesho

IndiaT04T07

  • Meesho charges no sales commission and earns from logistics fulfilment fees and advertising. V 2025-12-01 · stale

    relies on logistics fulfilment fees and advertising income Business Today

  • Meesho holds no inventory and relies on many sellers and logistics partners. V 2025-12-01 · stale

    Meesho does not hold inventory and relies on a broad seller base and third-party logistics partners. Business Today

I Lesson. A zero-commission marketplace can monetise through logistics and ads instead. Reasoning: removing commission lowers prices for value-focused buyers in smaller cities.

Transfer check. Fits value segments with many small sellers. Quality control is the stated weakness.

I Lesson. An open protocol does not create demand by itself. Reasoning: retail orders fell when incentives were reduced, and dispute handling was a stated gap.

Transfer check. Relevant to any interoperability or open-network proposal. Plan buyer demand and grievance handling first.

Alibaba instant commerce (Taobao Shangou)

ChinaT07T04

  • Alibaba announced a 50 billion yuan (US$7 billion) 12-month subsidy for consumers and merchants on its instant commerce service. V 2025-07-02 · stale

    store launch subsidies, product discounts, delivery subsidies, and reduced or waived commissions South China Morning Post

I Lesson. Instant commerce in China became a subsidy war between platforms. Reasoning: the subsidy covers launch, delivery and commission relief, competing with JD.com and Meituan.

Transfer check. Subsidy wars favour the deepest pockets. Do not model a challenger's economics on subsidised volumes.

Amazon Ads

North America, globalT05

  • Amazon's 2025 advertising revenue passed $68 billion. V 2026-02-06 · stale

    Amazon across 2025 saw ad revenue surpass $68 billion. Marketing Dive

  • Q4 2025 ad revenue was $21.3 billion, up 22% year on year. V 2026-02-06 · stale

    Revenue derived from ad sales rose 22% year over year to $21.3 billion for the quarter. Marketing Dive

I Lesson. Retail media at this scale is a second business model, not a feature. Reasoning: ad revenue of this size funds price and delivery investment that pure retailers cannot match.

Transfer check. Scale-dependent. A retailer with a small share of shopper traffic will not reach the same advertiser demand.

India quick commerce (Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart)

IndiaT07T04

  • India quick commerce grew from $300 million in 2022 to $7.1 billion by FY2025 (source for size not named). V 2025-05-05 · stale

    soaring from $300 million in 2022 to $7.1 billion by fiscal year 2025 Cornell SC Johnson College of Business

  • Blinkit aims to deliver within 10 minutes from dark stores. V 2025-05-05 · stale

    aiming to fulfill orders within 10 minutes Cornell SC Johnson College of Business

I Lesson. Dark stores close to dense demand make 10-minute delivery viable in Indian metros. Reasoning: growth concentrated in dense cities with low labour cost.

Transfer check. Three conditions decide transfer: order density, labour cost, and whether delivery platforms already own the customer. Compare with Getir in Europe.
